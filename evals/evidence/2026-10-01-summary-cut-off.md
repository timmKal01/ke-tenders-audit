## Summary

Reviewed 31 published awards for PC Kinyanjui Technical Training Institute. Red-flag scan found 8 single-bidder awards, 1 award signed before the tender closed, and 4 repeat winners at the same buyer. Price benchmarking identified one strong outlier: the ICT materials award at 2,898,800 KES is 7.14x the median of 5 comparables. Two single-bidder awards were flagged for committee attention. ## Findings

### F001. Price outlier

- **Finding:** The ICT materials award is a strong price outlier versus comparable awards in the same item group.
- **Evidence:** Awarded 2,898,800 KES to CAGE DYNAMICS; median of 5 comparables is 406,121.8 KES (Q1 365,295, Q3 984,900); ratio to median 7.14, flagged as outlier.
- **Source:** OCDS record `ocds-5whusi-302523-PCKTTI-112` (PPRA, tenders.go.ke)
- **Record says:** buyer PC KINYANJUI TECHNICAL TRAINING INSTITUTE; method open; winner(s) CAGE DYNAMICS; amount(s) KES 2,898,800.00; bidders listed 3
- **Approved by:** Timkev on 2026-10-01T17:05:47+00:00

### F002. Single bidder

- **Finding:** The groceries and dairy supply was awarded under an open method with only one bidder listed.
- **Evidence:** KERRYJAY ENTERPRISES is the only bidder on an open tender; the recorded amount is 0 KES, which is a data gap and prevents price benchmarking.
- **Source:** OCDS record `ocds-5whusi-302418-PCKTTI-146` (PPRA, tenders.go.ke)
- **Record says:** buyer PC KINYANJUI TECHNICAL TRAINING INSTITUTE; method open; winner(s) KERRYJAY ENTERPRISES; amount(s) KES 0 (data gap); bidders listed 1

### F003. Single bidder
